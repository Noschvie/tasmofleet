section: Changed
---
One word per result across the app: a finished success reads Succeeded (it was Completed, OK and Done in different places), a job that waits for a free slot reads Waiting (it was Queued), and a transaction whose devices ended differently says it in the same words as counts ("30 succeeded · 1 failed", "Succeeded on 3 of 5"). The user page's activity, the backups table and its restore list, the result of a device setting, the transaction page's progress line and cancel text, the bulk result and the firmware download badges follow, the firmware badges in sentence case (Cached, Failed, Fetching). Stored values do not change, and Refused, Not started and a device's Done step keep their words (#1105)
