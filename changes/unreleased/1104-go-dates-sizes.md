Dates that the server wrote into a sentence are now drawn like every other date, in the browser's own language, order and time zone: the release cards of a new update, the date of a release and of a cached file, the rule history (its day titles and the Revert dialog), where a value in a policy came from, the delete and restore confirmations of a backup, the "has not answered yet" banner of a device, the comparison of two backups, the restore and replace pages, a user's creation date and one-time password. The last-contact tooltip of a connection channel no longer shows a server-zone date and says how long ago instead. Sizes are written one way, in KiB and MiB, on the Firmware page, the update preview and the transaction page (#1104)
