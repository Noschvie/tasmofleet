section: Changed
---

Reads follow the same rule as changes: a device online on TasmoFleet's broker is read over MQTT first. The background read of the broker a device reports to (Status 6, SetOption3) and the identity check before it now go over MQTT first, and the restart reason the health tracker reads after a restart (Status 1) does too, so it is also read for a device whose HTTP is blocked or that refuses the stored web password. A foreign device that answers on a device's topic is logged as a warning. HTTP still carries a read when MQTT is not usable for the device or leaves the read unanswered, and where MQTT cannot do it: the health poll (it measures HTTP), the identity probe of "Refresh identity", of a newly added device and of the background identity refresh after every restart and every 24 hours (the firmware language is only on the device's web page; its other reads, Status 5 included, stay on HTTP with it for now, #418), the discovery scan, the files of a backup, and the replies the MQTT channel cannot match (`State`, a bare `Gpio`, `Gpios`, `Modules`). The list, with the Tasmota source that proves each case, is in the MQTT transport security notes (#839)
